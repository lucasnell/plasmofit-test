Single-unit simulations of cycle_length bias

plasmofit · SLURM 32423, 32426, 32428, 32432, 32435 · read 2026-10-23, 2026-10-26, 2026-10-27, and 2026-10-28 · corrected 2026-10-27: arm (a) changed the prior's width as well as its centre; 1675 (not 1677) of 1680 fits pass

Design

  • Unit: MMV048_PIB.1800 alone: 6 series, one shared trajectory, no hierarchy.
  • Truth: its posterior means from the real-data fit np_wide_total0_nc384_t: cycle_length 42.84 h, b_shape 20.10.
  • Schedules: real times to 168 h (34 obs), or the same series extended to 192 h (46 obs).
  • Data generated at n_c 384 or 1024. At 1024, b_shape is lowered to keep the total stage spread at 168 h unchanged.
  • Fit: n_c 384 always, with b_shape estimated or fixed at the truth. Production cycle_length prior: centred 48 h, sd 1.99 h; 3.8% of its mass lies below the truth.
  • Replicates and gate: 60 replicates per cell, 4 chains, max R-hat < 1.05. 2152 of 2160 fits pass across the main study and extensions (a)–(d).

Extensions, on the same noise draws:

  • (a) Prior arm: the main datasets refitted with the prior centred at the truth (42.84 h). Same sd_logit_cl (1), but that is 3.12 h sd in hours here against 1.99 h at 48 h, so the prior is also wider.
  • (b) Low-synchrony truth: b_shape 5.86, the lowest unit, instead of 20.10. Everything else unchanged, production prior.
  • (c) b_shape not mapped: at true n_c 1024, b_shape stays at 20.10 instead of being lowered to 9.54. Both true n_c then start equally synchronous, and 1024 desynchronises more slowly than the fit's 384 allows (stage spread at 168 h: 0.0995 against 0.1276 cycles sd). Only the 1024 cells are fitted; at 384 this arm is the main study. Production prior.
  • (d) Wider production prior: the main datasets refitted with the prior still centred at 48 h, widened from 1.99 to 2.99 h sd (sd_logit_cl 1.49).

Findings

Contrasts are paired: both fits use the same replicate's data (or the same noise draws) and both pass. Hours.

  • Biased long in every cell (+0.4 to +4.3 h). At the real 168 h schedule with b_shape estimated, the posterior is essentially the prior: mean 47.3 h and sd 1.9 h, against the prior's 47.4 h and 2.0 h.
  • The production prior is the largest source. Moving it to the truth shortens the estimate in every pair, by 1.0–2.7 h with b_shape estimated and 0.3–2.0 h with it fixed. This moves the centre (48 → 42.84 h) and the width in hours (1.99 → 3.12 h sd) together.
  • Mostly the centre, not the width (d). Widening the prior by a similar amount at the same 48 h centre (1.99 → 2.99 h sd) moves the estimate by only +0.04 to −0.41 h. Approximate: the width effect is measured at 48 h, not at the truth. At the real 168 h schedule with b_shape estimated the posterior still sits near the prior: median 47.5 h against the wide prior's mean of 46.9 h.
  • A long residual remains with the prior at the truth. This comes from the likelihood and nuisances, not the prior:
    • +1.3 to +1.8 h with b_shape estimated;
    • +0.4 to +0.6 h with it fixed and n_c right;
    • +1.6 to +1.7 h with it fixed and a true n_c of 1024.
  • Estimating b_shape adds +1.6 to +2.1 h under the production prior and +0.9 to +1.1 h with the prior at the truth, when n_c is right.
  • The longer window shortens the estimate by 1.0–2.0 h under the production prior. With the prior at the truth this falls to −0.5 to +0.2 h, so the window effect is mostly the extra data resisting the prior.
  • Low synchrony stays long (+1.0 to +3.6 h). Its posterior sds are 1.5–2.3 h, about the prior's, so these data inform cycle_length even less. The b_shape cost (+0.3 to +1.3 h) and the n_c effect (−0.6 to +0.5 h) are both smaller than at the main truth.
  • A truth that desynchronises more slowly than the fit allows (c) pushes the estimate longer, more so at 192 h. Against the main study at true n_c 384 (same b_shape, same noise): +2.1 to +2.4 h at 192 h; at 168 h, +1.0 h with b_shape fixed and 0.0 h with it estimated.
    • The longer window no longer shortens the estimate: 192 − 168 h is +0.1 h (estimated) and −0.1 h (fixed), against −1.0 to −2.0 h in the main study.
    • With b_shape estimated the posterior is the prior at both windows (median 47.3 h).
    • Fixing b_shape at the true 20.10 is no longer the right nuisance value, since the fitted n_c is wrong.

Can the data pick out the less-biased fits?

Exploratory; no reading rule fixed in advance. Paired on identical data, both fits passing. Fixed b_shape and the truth-centred prior are the less-biased fits. elpd = PSIS-LOO expected log predictive density, summed over the unit's 34 or 46 observations; higher = predicts held-out observations better. “Clearly” = beyond 2 se of that pair's own pointwise difference. log BF = log Bayes factor (marginal likelihood ratio); under 1 is a factor under 2.7.

  • Sampler diagnostics: no. Divergences 0.07–0.38% and no iteration at the treedepth cap in every cell. Fixed fits take fewer leapfrog steps (65–87 against 75–102 per iteration) because they have one parameter fewer, not because they are right.
  • Fixed at truth vs estimated, elpd: leans toward fixed on average but rarely decides a replicate.
    • Main truth, production prior: mean gain +0.05 to +1.20; fixed predicts better in 36–48 of 58–60 pairs, clearly in 8–13. Per-pair se 0.5–1.6.
    • Low-synchrony truth: points the wrong way. Fixed is less biased in 40–54 of 60 pairs, but the mean gain is −0.01 to −0.34.
    • The size of the elpd gain does not track the size of the bias difference (correlations −0.48 to +0.66, no consistent sign).
    • Savage–Dickey log BF, fixed : estimated, production prior: −0.21 to +0.32 in cell means (low synchrony at true n_c 1024: +0.62, +0.79).
  • Truth-centred vs production prior:
    • elpd favours the truth-centred prior in 51–59 of 60 pairs (mean +0.24 to +0.96), clearly in only 0–12.
    • log BF, truth-centred : production, is negative at the real 168 h schedule with b_shape estimated (−0.23, −0.14; positive in 18 of 60). The data mildly prefer the 48 h prior that gives 2.5–2.7 h more bias. Either the likelihood peaking long or the truth-centred prior being wider in hours could cause this; the design cannot separate them. Elsewhere −0.03 to +1.34.
  • Overall: even with the truth among the candidates, these measures lean toward the less-biased fit over 60 replicates, rarely decide one dataset, and twice lean the other way. None would flag a single fit as less biased.

Limits

  • One unit, 6 series, no hierarchy. In the full model, 13 trials share the cycle_length prior's centre, so the pull on any one trial differs.
  • A prior centred at the truth is a diagnostic only.
  • None of these numbers is a correction for the full-model 41.82 h.
