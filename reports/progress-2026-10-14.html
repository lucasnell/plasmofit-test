plasmofit — progress, 30 September to 14 October 2026

Within-host dynamics of Plasmodium falciparum from volunteer infection studies. Question: what is the asexual blood-stage cycle length, and how much of the estimate comes from modelling assumptions rather than the data?

Status as of 2026-10-14. Data: _data/wockner-cleaned.csv, 1130 observations, 177 series, 13 trials. Sources for every number are in the last section.

Bottom line
  1. The cycle-length estimate moved from 43.7–45.5 h to about 41 h, and the move is explained. It came from n_c, the number of developmental stages per cycle. n_c had been set for numerical accuracy; it turns out to set how fast parasites desynchronise, and the value in use (96) is wrong by the data, with or without priors.
  2. At the settings now considered defensible, every estimate lies between 40.9 and 41.8 h (mean over 13 trials). No number is final: fits at n_c = 384 do not converge reliably, and two trials show two regions of the posterior about 3 h apart.
  3. The age-structure question is closed. The data constrain only the total spread of parasite ages at the end of the observation window, not how it splits between spread present at inoculation and spread acquired later. Most of it is present at inoculation. A continuous-age model (IPM) would not be identified, so it will not be built.
  4. Fits run 12× faster (38.5 h to 3.2 h for the same fit) after pinning each chain to its own cache group, which is what makes the open convergence questions cheap to answer.

2. Major advances

1 – 2 Oct

Fixing synchrony beats estimating it

b_shape (the shape of the initial stage distribution, i.e. synchrony at inoculation) predicts better fixed than estimated: +28 to +36 elpd at every value tried, 10–12 standard errors, at n_c 96. Gains plateau at 400, a ~4 h spread of starting ages.

wockner-bshape-ladder.R → bshape-ladder.log

2 – 6 Oct

The simulated bias is attributed

Of the +1.97 h by which the estimator overstates a simulated truth, estimating b_shape carries ~0.92 h, the likelihood itself +0.41 h, and the cycle-length prior's centre ~0.44 h. Six other explanations were tested and refuted: phase volume, phase–period compensation, bound geometry, posterior median vs mean, sd_iRBC, and the joint mode (MAP).

wockner-schedule-sim-analyze.R → schedsim-analyze-2026-10-05.txt

6 – 8 Oct

n_c is a biological assumption, and 96 is wrong

n_c fixes the desynchronisation rate. Without any priors, 192 beats 96 in 14 of 14 trial×inoculum units, +72.4 log-likelihood units summed. Moving 96 → 192 shifts cycle_length by −2.17 h, more than the whole simulated bias. In simulation, misspecifying n_c twofold shifts it by 1.0–1.5 h, in both directions.

decay-law-read.R → decay-law-read-2026-10-08.txt; nc-ladder-read.R → nc-ladder-2026-10-06.txt; nc-2x2-read.R → nc-2x2-2026-10-07.txt

7 Oct

Why n_c moves the cycle length

Parasites sequester at 18.58 h, so only ~40% of each cycle is visible in blood. As the age distribution broadens, the visible part's centre drifts, so the period seen in the data is not the cycle_length parameter. At n_c 96 the visible period runs ~0.75 h short, and a fit inflates cycle_length to compensate. Deterministic, no estimation involved.

nc-period-check.R → nc-period-check-2026-10-07.txt

8 – 9 Oct

Age structure: the data see only the total spread

Across n_c values, the spread acquired during the window varies 2.0× while the total at the last observation varies 12.7%; b_shape compensates in 14 of 14 units (Spearman −1.00). Roughly 0.11 cycles of spread are present at inoculation against 0.05–0.08 acquired. This one result explains why the decay law (√ vs linear) could not be distinguished, why the n_c profile has no lower bound on dispersion, and why b_shape is unidentified. Consequence: do not build the IPM; it would return its dispersion rate at zero.

nc-profile-fast-read.R → nc-profile-fast-2026-10-09.txt; claude/ipm-decision.md

9 Oct

Forward map checked, and left as it was

A convolution form of the stage chain was added to the package, measured through the real likelihood, and removed: 2.8–5× slower and less accurate at high n_c. What it left behind: two independently written forward maps agree to 6.4e-14 on log_prob, so the production series is now independently verified.

package commits 815f3a0 → bba580f; use-conv-switch-check-2026-10-09.txt

4 – 5 Oct

Hierarchy: no robust evidence either way

The held-out test favouring the hierarchy (pooling trials) reversed when the mask changed (−3.27 at the last third, −0.04 at the last quarter), and the horizon ladder is flat. The earlier claim that the hierarchy earns its keep is withdrawn. Closed unless a design argument changes.

wockner-designA-score.R → designA-score.log, designQ-score.log

13 Oct

Fits 12× faster

The same model ran at 89–345 ms per gradient depending on what else of ours shared the node. Chains sharing a 16 MB L3 cache run at half speed. Running each chain as its own process pinned to one cache group cut a fit from 38.5 h to 3.2 h with identical log_prob. Now the standard driver.

4. Terms and sources

cycle_length
Duration of the asexual blood-stage cycle, hours; one value per trial.
n_c
Number of developmental compartments per cycle. Stage spread after k cycles is cycle_length·√(k/n_c), so it sets the desynchronisation rate.
b_shape
Shape of the starting stage distribution; higher means more synchronous at inoculation.
gate
Convergence rule: max R-hat < 1.05 over monitored quantities. A run that fails is not reported.
elpd
Expected log predictive density, PSIS-LOO over the 1130 observations; positive differences favour the first model.
IPM
Integral projection model: continuous parasite age instead of discrete compartments.
