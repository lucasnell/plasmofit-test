2. PARK ET AL. (2020), DIRECT SINGLE-CELL, 3D7HT-GFP
n_c is implied by the PER-CYCLE part (sibling, sigma_k) alone. The founder
part (sigma_i) is persistent lineage heterogeneity the model cannot express;
it is shown only to say how much of the total it is.

   synchronized rep 1   tau 54.0 h  sigma_k 1.7 h (CV  3.15%) -> n_c   1009 | sigma_i 2.9 h | total 3.4 h (CV  6.30%) -> n_c   252
   unsynchronized rep 1 tau 57.5 h  sigma_k 5.0 h (CV  8.70%) -> n_c    132 | sigma_i 4.9 h | total 7.0 h (CV 12.17%) -> n_c    67
   synchronized rep 2   tau 57.3 h  sigma_k 2.7 h (CV  4.71%) -> n_c    450 | sigma_i 2.7 h | total 3.8 h (CV  6.63%) -> n_c   227

   Their standard-culture control (Fig. S4, ring emergence after one cycle):
   48.7 +/- 5.0 h, CV 10.27% -> n_c    95, but that spread also carries the
   synchronisation's own width, so it bounds n_c from below only.


3. SMITH ET AL. (2020), TABLE S6 — PERSISTENT heterogeneity, NOT n_c
Each parasite keeps one period, so this spread grows linearly in cycles and
is not 1 / sqrt(n_c). Listed for the record, with the n_c it WOULD imply if
(wrongly) read as per-cycle. Model error is their own fit error, for scale.

   3D7    mean period 39 h  CoV  2.760%  (model error 1.160)  [would imply n_c     1313]

   3D7 at +/- 1 h on its mean period: CoV 3.65% (38 h), 2.76% (39 h), 4.20% (40 h).
   3D7 has the LARGEST model error of the five strains (1.16-1.59 against
   0.168-0.647), so it is the strain their model fits worst.


4. WOCKNER ET AL. (2020), the source data
   Sine-wave life cycle 38.8 h (95% CI 38.3-39.2) overall; by subject mean
   39.5 h, sd 4.04 over 128 subjects, range 30.6-55.0 (Supplementary Table 5).
   That sd is BETWEEN HOSTS, not between parasites, so it anchors neither.
   Samples were run in duplicate or triplicate and GEOMETRICALLY AVERAGED on
   the log10 scale before release, and Supplementary Table 2 holds only the
   averages, so the replicate spread that would anchor sd_iRBC is not in it.
