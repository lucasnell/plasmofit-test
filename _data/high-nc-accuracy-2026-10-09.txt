VERDICT: mat_exp_series is stable -- halving its step count, which also
changes the matrix it exponentiates, moves it by ~1e-16 and does NOT
grow with n_c. So conv_series is what drifts, and the project reading of
max_rel_diff (findings.md: 8e-13, 1.4e-12, 2.8e-12 at n_c 96/192/384) as
the SERIES degrading rather than the reference stands.

Why: an FFT convolution has an error floor of ~eps * max|u| in ABSOLUTE
terms, while the observable at a trough is a small sum of small positive
terms. As n_c rises the age distribution narrows and the troughs deepen,
so that fixed absolute floor becomes a larger relative error exactly
where the observable is smallest. mat_exp_series propagates the state
vector directly, with no global transform, so small entries keep their
relative accuracy.
