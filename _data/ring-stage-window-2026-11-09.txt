3. THE WINDOW FOR THE STARTING AGE DISTRIBUTION.
   The two sources agree to within ~1 h on a 48 h cycle. At the cycle
   lengths these data fit, the ring window is:

   cycle_length 39.40 h: Smith 0-12.3 h, Llinas (ring < 2%) 0-12.8 h
   cycle_length 41.00 h: Smith 0-12.8 h, Llinas (ring < 2%) 0-13.3 h
   cycle_length 42.84 h: Smith 0-13.4 h, Llinas (ring < 2%) 0-13.9 h
   cycle_length 45.60 h: Smith 0-14.2 h, Llinas (ring < 2%) 0-14.8 h

   Against the CIRCULATING window the design currently uses (24 h at a
   48 h cycle; half-sequestration 18.58 h, 5% circulating 24.3 h), the
   ring window is about 60-65% as wide.
