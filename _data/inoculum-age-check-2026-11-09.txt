5. THE INOCULUM'S SPREAD IS ITSELF A PRODUCT OF n_c
   The donor's blood stage ran k_donor cycles before collection (first PCR
   detection day 5.5, or ~7.5 if the day-2 bites dominated, to collection day
   13.5-14: 6-8.5 days, ~3.5-5 cycles). Under the Erlang chain that alone
   gives a per-lineage age variance of k_donor / n_c cycles^2, before any
   spread in liver release (which adds) and before the circulating window and
   freezing thin the old tail (which removes). If the start carried at least
   that variance, a unit whose total variance at 168 h is V (from the fit, the
   quantity the data constrain, 2026-10-09) needs
       n_c >= (k_donor + 168 / cycle_length) / V.
   A soft bound, not a strict one; per-lineage, not population (see
   unitsim-spread-check.R).
